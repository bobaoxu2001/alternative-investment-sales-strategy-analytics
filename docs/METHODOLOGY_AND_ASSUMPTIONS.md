# Methodology and Assumptions

**Project:** Alternative Investment Sales Strategy Analytics Platform  
**Author:** Allen Xu

This document explains the analytical methodology, modeling assumptions, and interpretation guardrails behind the project. The goal is to make the portfolio project transparent, reproducible, and credible for a sales strategy analytics audience.

## 1. Project Objective

The project simulates the analytics layer a Global Client Solutions / Wealth Solutions sales strategy team might use to understand:

- where the alternative investment sales funnel drops off
- which relationship managers are most productive
- which products and asset classes show strongest demand
- which campaigns produce the best modeled efficiency
- which advisors should be prioritized for next-quarter coverage

The project is designed to demonstrate analytical structure and business judgment, not to represent actual KKR, client, advisor, fund, or CRM data.

## 2. Synthetic Data Methodology

All data is generated by `src/generate_synthetic_data.py` using a fixed random seed (`42`). The generator creates seven raw tables:

- advisors
- relationship managers
- funds
- sales activities
- opportunities
- campaigns
- campaign engagement

The synthetic data is designed with directional business relationships rather than random independent rows. Examples:

- Larger AUM segments are more likely to produce larger expected commitments.
- Prior alternative investment experience influences engagement and conversion behavior.
- Higher activity engagement increases the likelihood of later-stage opportunities.
- Fund asset class, advisor type, and region influence opportunity patterns.
- Campaign engagement can source opportunities and modeled attributable commitments.

## 3. Analytics Pipeline

The workflow has four main steps:

1. Generate deterministic synthetic raw data.
2. Load raw CSVs into SQLite for SQL analysis.
3. Run the pandas analysis pipeline to produce dashboard-ready marts.
4. Generate executive charts from processed outputs.

The Python pipeline and SQL scripts are intentionally parallel: the SQL files mirror the same core business questions as the pandas pipeline so the project demonstrates both technical styles.

## 4. Key Assumptions

- The dataset represents a trailing 12-month sales cycle from May 2025 through April 2026.
- `actual_commitment` is populated only for committed opportunities.
- Campaign ROI uses a simplified 2.5% management-fee proxy.
- Campaign attribution is modeled and should be interpreted directionally.
- Advisor priority scoring is rule-based, not machine-learned.
- Priority tiers are relative buckets within the synthetic advisor universe.

## 5. ROI Interpretation

The campaign ROI outputs are intentionally included because ROI analysis is central to sales enablement and distribution strategy. However, the magnitude of modeled multiples should not be interpreted as real-world benchmarks.

Because the data is synthetic, the campaign ROI values are best read as relative channel-efficiency signals. The analytical framework matters more than the absolute modeled multiple.

## 6. Advisor Priority Scoring

The advisor score combines:

- engagement
- AUM segment
- conversion probability
- product fit
- recency

The score is meant to show how a sales strategy team could build an explainable next-best-action framework. In production, the rule-based score should be replaced or calibrated with historical closed/lost outcomes and a predictive model.

## 7. Limitations

- Synthetic data cannot capture all real-world distribution frictions.
- No true CRM history, fee schedules, fund capacity constraints, or competitor data is included.
- Campaign attribution is simplified.
- The priority score is explainable but not statistically optimized.

## 8. Production Extensions

In a production analytics environment, the next steps would be:

- connect Salesforce or HubSpot activity and opportunity data
- join fund administration and fee schedule data
- add row-level security for relationship manager dashboards
- create a learned conversion model for advisor and opportunity scoring
- deploy the dashboard to Tableau Server or Power BI Service
